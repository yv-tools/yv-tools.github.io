Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

YouTube Viralizer is a private tool operated by Mahdiyar Afsari. It helps prepare, upload and track videos on YouTube channels we manage, and is used only with the permission of each channel's owner.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, channel owners agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access and why

  • With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), we use the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read video and channel statistics. We may use the YouTube Analytics API to read the channel's performance.
  • We use Google Drive only to read the video files shared with us for upload, and to store the thumbnails the app itself creates.
  • We do not access the owner's email, contacts or any other Google data.

How we store it

  • Access permissions (tokens) are stored encrypted. Video statistics are stored to measure performance and are refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as YouTube's policies require.
  • We do not use cookies.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties. Data is used only to operate the connected channels.

Your choices

  • Channel owners can revoke access at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • To have your data deleted, email mahdiyar.afsari@gmail.com. We delete it within 7 days.
