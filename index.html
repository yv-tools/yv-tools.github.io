YouTube Viralizer

YouTube Viralizer is a private tool that helps us prepare, upload and track videos on the YouTube channels we manage, always with the channel owners' permission.

What it does

  • Research: studies the best-performing public videos in a niche to learn which titles, thumbnails and topics work.
  • Preparation: picks up new videos from a shared Google Drive folder and drafts their titles, descriptions, hashtags and thumbnail options for a person to review and approve.
  • Publishing: uploads approved videos to the owner's channel through the official YouTube Data API.
  • Tracking: follows each video's public statistics and flags videos that need attention.

Who it is for

It is not a public service. Only channels whose owners have explicitly connected them through Google sign-in are used, and owners can disconnect at any time.
